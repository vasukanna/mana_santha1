Privacy Policy for Santhalo

Effective Date: October 21, 2026 • Website: https://santhalo.com

1. Introduction

Welcome to Santhalo ("we", "our", or "us"). Santhalo is an agricultural and rural community marketplace connecting farmers, traders, livestock owners, and rural buyers across Andhra Pradesh. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

2. Information We Collect

When you register, create an account, or post listings on Santhalo, we may collect:

  • Account Details: Name, email address, and profile picture provided through Google Sign-In or manual account creation.
  • Contact Information: Mobile phone number (required to allow prospective buyers to call or message you on WhatsApp regarding your marketplace listings).
  • Location Information: District, mandal, and village or locality name so buyers can view nearby rural produce, livestock, and equipment.
  • User-Generated Content (UGC): Listing titles, descriptions, pricing, photos taken with camera or uploaded from gallery, and video tour clips.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to:

  • Operate and display your marketplace listings to interested buyers.
  • Facilitate direct peer-to-peer contact between farmers and buyers via telephone, WhatsApp, or in-app chat.
  • Prevent spam, abuse, fraudulent listings, and ensure compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the application:

  • Go to Profile → Scroll to Delete Account / ఖాతా తొలగించండి.
  • Confirming deletion immediately and permanently deletes:
    • Your profile and user authentication account.
    • All active and archived listings posted by you in Firestore.
    • All uploaded photos, videos, and associated chats.
  • You can also request immediate manual deletion by emailing vasu.kannaluri@gmail.com.

5. User-Generated Content (UGC) Safety, Reporting & Blocking

Santhalo enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against abusive, illegal, fraudulent, or objectionable content. In accordance with Google Play Store UGC requirements:

  • In-App Reporting: Every listing and chat thread provides a visible Report (ఫిర్యాదు) button to flag spam, scam, offensive, or inappropriate content. Reported items are reviewed by our moderation team within 24 hours.
  • In-App User Blocking: Users can tap Block User (బ్లాక్ చేయండి) on any listing or chat to immediately hide that user's listings and prevent all future communication.
  • Violating accounts are banned immediately and prohibited from the platform.

6. Third-Party Services & Data Security

We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to securely store credentials and marketplace data. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties or advertising brokers.
